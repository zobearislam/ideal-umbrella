Ideal Umbrella Controls

Fly: Joystick / WASD · slider or ▲▼ thrust · ↑↓ pitch · Q/E roll · X or Stop.

Dock: Near a planet/moon, press Dock for market, craft, and missions.

Ships: Combat hulls have turrets and missiles; cargo hulls do not.

Economy: Rival NPC companies haul goods across systems. Buy them out from Transport (expensive).

Tip: Open Transport → Rival Companies to see live NPC deliveries and buyouts.
