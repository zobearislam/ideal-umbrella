Space Pack Controls

Fly mode: Joystick / WASD / arrows to turn. W/S or slider for speed. Q/E roll. X stop.

Walk mode: Move inside ship. Green door = airlock. Front = seat.

EVA: Free movement outside. Stay close to re-enter.

Lock: Follows a body. Go jumps near selected object.

Orbits: Toggle orbital paths. Pause freezes time.

Gates: Fly into the cyan ring gates to jump to the connected gate.

Factions: Stations belong to Sol Federation, Alpha Cooperative, Sirius Mining Corp, or Independent. Reputation is tracked per faction.

Dock: Near a station, press Dock to buy ships, hire pilots, and craft resources.

Laser: Spacebar or Laser button fires abstract defense laser. Affects reputation.

Economy: Credits buy ships. Reputation changes prices and rises with peaceful actions.

Tip: Lock Earth or ISS and accelerate time to watch orbits.
