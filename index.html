Space Pack Controls

Fly mode: Joystick / WASD / arrows to turn. W/S or slider for speed. Q/E roll. X stop.

Walk mode: Move inside ship. Green door = airlock. Front = seat.

Dock: Near a planet/moon, press Dock for station services, sell cargo, craft, and complete missions.

Shared economy: Personal & company credits, inventory, and reputation sync with Transport and Missions pages.

Loop: Accept a mission → gather or craft cargo → dock and complete · or run Transport deliveries → goods land in system warehouses → sell at a station.

Laser: Spacebar fires abstract defense laser (can affect reputation via IAG).

Tip: Start near Earth, Dock, sell iron, then open Transport for fleet contracts.
