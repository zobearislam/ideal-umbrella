# Content Expansion - Ideal Umbrella

Major content expansion for the Space Pack / Ideal Umbrella project.

## New / Expanded Data (v0.9)

### `js/data/expansion_v09.js`
- **45 additional resources**: rare earths (scandium, yttrium, europium, gadolinium, terbium, dysprosium, holmium, erbium, thulium, ytterbium, lutetium, praseodymium, samarium), radioactives (promethium, actinium, thorium, plutonium, neptunium, americium, curium), refined (superconductor, metamaterial, smart alloy, photonic crystal, graphene, nanotube, aerogel, plasma core, gravitic lens, neural interface), and high-tier (dark matter core, chroniton particle, zero point module, precursor crystal, living metal, void crystal, stellar core fragment, quantum entanglement key, antimatter stabilizer, bio-neural matrix, phase matter, tachyon emitter, reality anchor, cosmic string, singularity core)
- **15 new ship types**: Prime Dreadnought, Colony Ark, Phase Assassin, Mobile Foundry, Ghost Frigate, Quantum Freighter, Planetary Siege Platform, Research University, Corsair Flagship, Diplomatic Flagship, Asteroid Processor, Anomaly Explorer, Stealth Hauler, Titan Carrier, Void Harvester
- **10 new factions**: Stellar Empire, Free Colonies Alliance, Tech Syndicate, Order of Void Walkers, Mineral Consortium, Scientific Federation, Merchant Republic, Shadow Council, Bio-Engineers Guild, Anomaly Hunters
- **20 additional extreme/high-tier mission templates** involving the new content

### Previous expansions
- `js/data/expansion_v08.js`: +30 resources, +12 ships, +8 factions, +12 missions
- `js/data/expansion_v07.js`: +22 resources, +10 ships, +6 factions, +8 missions
- `js/data/resources.js` + `js/data/additional_content.js`: base content
- `js/core/universe.js`: 35+ star systems with full route connectivity
- `js/data/missions.js` + `js/data/missions_extra.js`: mission templates

## Integration

Include `expansion_v09.js` after the core data files (it is added to `index.html`, `missions.html`, and `transport.html`).

All data remains attached to `window` so classic scripts continue to work.
