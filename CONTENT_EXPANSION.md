# Content Expansion - Ideal Umbrella

Major content expansion for the Space Pack / Ideal Umbrella project.

## New / Expanded Data (v1.1)

### `js/data/expansion_v11.js`
- **40+ additional resources**: rare earth ores (scandium, yttrium, neodymium, dysprosium, thorium), refined materials (rare earth alloy, neodymium magnet, superconducting wire, photonic crystal, metamaterial), and extreme high-tier (dark matter crystal, antimatter fuel cell, gravity lens, time crystal, quantum computer core, neural mesh, living alloy, singularity core, omega stabilizer, chronal engine, phase drive, reality anchor, Absolute Omega Particle, Precursor Omega Archive, Living Dreadnought Core, Singularity Dreadnought Engine, Dark Omega Reactor, Omega Core)
- **15 new ship types**: Absolute Dreadnought, Genesis Ark, Phase Overlord, Mega Forge World, Shadow Overlord, Omega Overlord, Titan Carrier, Knowledge Ark, Pirate Empire Flagship, Diplomatic Overlord, Void Overlord, Chronal Overlord, Neural Overlord, Bio Overlord, Research University Ship
- **12 new factions**: Absolute Order, Genesis Collective, Phase Lords, Forge Empire, Shadow Empire, Omega Empire, Titan Legion, Knowledge Keepers, Pirate Empire, Diplomatic Order, Void Empire, Chronal Empire
- **20 additional extreme/high-tier mission templates** involving the new content

### Previous expansions
- `js/data/expansion_v10.js`: +55 resources, +20 ships, +15 factions, +30 missions
- `js/data/expansion_v09.js`: +45 resources, +15 ships, +10 factions, +20 missions
- `js/data/expansion_v08.js`: +30 resources, +12 ships, +8 factions, +12 missions
- `js/data/expansion_v07.js`: +22 resources, +10 ships, +6 factions, +8 missions
- `js/data/resources.js` + `js/data/additional_content.js`: base content
- `js/core/universe.js`: 35+ star systems with full route connectivity
- `js/data/missions.js` + `js/data/missions_extra.js`: mission templates

## Integration

Include `expansion_v11.js` after the core data files (it is added to `index.html`, `missions.html`, and `transport.html`).

All data remains attached to `window` so classic scripts continue to work.
