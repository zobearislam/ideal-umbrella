# Content Expansion - Ideal Umbrella

Major content expansion for the Space Pack / Ideal Umbrella project.

## New / Expanded Data (v1.2)

### `js/data/expansion_v12.js`
- **50+ additional resources**: exotic raw materials (promethium, einsteinium, void ore, phase crystal, chronal ore, neural ore, bio-essence, singularity dust, dark matter ore), refined materials (quantum fabric, living circuit, phase alloy, chronal stabilizer, neural processor, void steel, antimatter containment, reality thread, precursor alloy, omega circuit), and ultimate high-tier (Absolute Singularity, Genesis Seed, Phase Emperor Core, Reality Warper, Omega Antimatter, Chronal God Engine, Neural God Core, Living Universe Core, Void God Crystal, Precursor God Archive, Absolute Omega Core, Reality Emperor Engine, Omega Reality Particle, and more)
- **20 new ship types**: Reality Ark, Phase Emperor, Omega Forge World, Shadow Emperor, Singularity Emperor, Chronal Emperor, Neural Godship, Living Universe Ship, Void Emperor, Absolute Dreadnought Prime, Genesis Emperor Ark, Phase God, Omega Reality Forge, Shadow God, Precursor Flagship, Chronal Godship, Neural Emperor, Bio Emperor, Void God, Absolute Emperor
- **15 new factions**: Reality Order, Genesis Empire, Phase Emperor's Court, Singularity Empire, Chronal God Order, Neural God Collective, Living Universe Cult, Void God Empire, Precursor Remnant, Absolute Emperor Order, Omega Reality Cult, Shadow God Council, Bio-God Empire, Dark Omega Order, Reality Emperor's Court
- **25 additional ultimate/extreme mission templates** involving the new content

### Previous expansions
- `js/data/expansion_v11.js`: +40 resources, +15 ships, +12 factions, +20 missions

- `js/data/expansion_v09.js`: +45 resources, +15 ships, +10 factions, +20 missions

- `js/core/universe.js`: 35+ star systems with full route connectivity
- `js/data/missions.js` + `js/data/missions_extra.js`: mission templates

## Integration

Include `expansion_v12.js` after the core data files (it is added to `index.html`, `missions.html`, and `transport.html`).

All data remains attached to `window` so classic scripts continue to work.
