# Content Expansion - Ideal Umbrella

Major content expansion for the Ideal Umbrella project.

## New / Expanded Data (v1.0)

### `js/data/expansion_v10.js`
- **55 additional resources**: exotic raw materials (molybdenum, tungsten, vanadium, chromium, manganese, cobalt, nickel, iridium, osmium, ruthenium, palladium), ices (helium-3, deuterium, methane clathrate, ammonia, enriched water), refined (high-entropy alloy, ceramic matrix, diamondoid, fullerene, quantum dot, meta-lens, room-temp superconductor, neutronium alloy, phase alloy, bio-steel), and extreme high-tier (strange matter, negative mass, tachyon crystal, chronal stabilizer, reality thread, void pearl, stellar plasma bottle, quantum foam, precursor key, living hull matrix, antimatter crystal, graviton emitter, entropy engine, refined cosmic dust, dark energy cell, phase shifter, neural lace, singularity seed, omega particle, chroniton lattice, void alloy plate, quantum entangler, bio-processor, stellar core alloy, precursor circuit, living metal ingot, tachyon coil, reality stabilizer, cosmic string segment, singularity engine)
- **20 new ship types**: Apex Dreadnought, World Ark, Phase Wraith, Mega Foundry, Shadow Cruiser, Quantum Superhauler, Orbital Fortress, Academy Ship, Pirate Mothership, Embassy Ship, Asteroid Cracker, Anomaly Hunter, Ghost Hauler, Leviathan Carrier, Void Reaper, Chronal Explorer, Singularity Tug, Neural Flagship, Bio Ark, Omega Dreadnought
- **15 new factions**: Imperial Remnant, Frontier League, Quantum Order, Void Reapers, Stellar Foundries, Chronal Institute, Free Merchants Guild, Shadow Syndicate, Bio Collective Prime, Anomaly Consortium, Stellar Empire Core, Research Exodus, Mineral Lords, Neural Collective, Omega Cult
- **30 additional extreme/high-tier mission templates** involving the new content

### Previous expansions
- `js/data/expansion_v09.js`: +45 resources, +15 ships, +10 factions, +20 missions
- `js/data/expansion_v08.js`: +30 resources, +12 ships, +8 factions, +12 missions
- `js/data/expansion_v07.js`: +22 resources, +10 ships, +6 factions, +8 missions
- `js/data/resources.js` + `js/data/additional_content.js`: base content
- `js/core/universe.js`: 35+ star systems with full route connectivity
- `js/data/missions.js` + `js/data/missions_extra.js`: mission templates

## Integration

Include `expansion_v10.js` after the core data files (it is added to `index.html`, `missions.html`, and `transport.html`).

All data remains attached to `window` so classic scripts continue to work.
